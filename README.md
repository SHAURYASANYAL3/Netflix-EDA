# Netflix Movies & TV Shows - Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset as part of the Pluto Academy Internship Project 01.

## Objectives
- Understand dataset structure
- Handle missing values
- Perform data cleaning
- Explore content trends
- Generate visualizations (Bar chart, Line chart, Histogram, Scatter plot, Pie chart, Heatmap)
- Derive business and research insights

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Insights
1. **Content Strategy Focus**: Movies (~69%) dominate the Netflix platform compared to TV Shows (~31%), suggesting Netflix historically prioritized licensing and producing feature-length films over episodic series.
2. **Global Production Hubs**: The United States is by far the largest producer of Netflix content, followed distantly by India, highlighting Netflix's strong focus on the US market and its significant investment in Bollywood and Indian regional content.
3. **Rapid Growth Era**: There was a massive exponential surge in content additions starting around 2015, peaking in 2019, which aligns with Netflix's aggressive global expansion and original content strategy during those years.
4. **Recent Content Bias**: The vast majority of movies available on the platform were released in the last decade (post-2010), showing a preference for newer, modern content rather than classics.
5. **Duration Trends**: While standard movie durations typically range between 90 and 120 minutes, there are many outliers (short documentaries and very long epics), but the average duration has remained relatively consistent over the decades.

## Most Surprising Finding
The most surprising finding was the steep drop-off in content added after 2019. While one might expect continuous exponential growth, the data clearly shows a plateau and decline, likely reflecting the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on global production schedules or a shift in strategy from quantity to quality.
